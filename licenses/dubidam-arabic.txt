The free personal-use edition draws a watermark in place of the Arabic-Indic
digits, the Arabic semicolon and question mark, quotation marks, dashes and
some symbols. The theme's unicode-range (assets/css/fonts.css) therefore covers
only the letters and marks the font draws properly, and Arslan Wessam supplies
the rest. A public site that sells a book needs NamelaType's commercial
licence, which also removes the watermark. Check the terms with NamelaType
before the site goes public.
