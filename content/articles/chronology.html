The timeline below follows the chronology in the book, which draws on the standard biographies of Paul and on the Roman historians. All dates are approximate and in the Common Era. Estimates of the date of Paul's death run from 62 to 67 CE.

  • c. 5–10 CE Born in Tarsus, Cilicia, a Jew of the tribe of Benjamin (allegedly), with Roman citizenship. [paulus_link slug="who-was-paul-of-tarsus" /]
  • c. 20–30 CE Studies the Torah under Gamaliel in Jerusalem and becomes a Pharisee. [paulus_link slug="who-was-paul-of-tarsus" anchor="s-under-gamaliel" /]
  • c. 33–36 CE Takes part in the persecution of the Jerusalem community. Reports his vision on the road to Damascus. [paulus_link slug="saul-the-persecutor" /] · [paulus_link slug="the-damascus-road" /]
  • c. 36–37 CE In Damascus and Arabia. [paulus_link slug="a-self-appointed-apostle" /]
  • c. 38 CE Flees to Jerusalem, meets Peter and James, then returns to Tarsus. [paulus_link slug="james-the-brother" /]
  • c. 39–43 CE Preaches in Syria and Cilicia, based in Tarsus.
  • c. 44 CE Brought to Antioch by Barnabas, where the disciples are first called Christians. [paulus_link slug="paul-the-father-of-a-new-religion" /]
  • c. 45 CE Carries famine relief to Jerusalem with Barnabas.
  • c. 47–48 CE First mission, to Cyprus, Pisidian Antioch, Iconium, Lystra and Derbe. [paulus_link slug="the-roman" /]
  • c. 50 CE The Jerusalem council on Gentile converts and the law. The confrontation with Peter at Antioch follows. [paulus_link slug="paul-against-the-disciples" /]
  • c. 51–53 CE Second mission, from Galatia into Greece. Writes to the Thessalonians from Corinth. [paulus_link slug="the-foundations-of-pauline-doctrine" /]
  • c. 54–56 CE Third mission, based at Ephesus. [paulus_link slug="by-their-fruits" /]
  • c. 57 CE In Macedonia and Corinth. Writes to the Corinthians and the Galatians. [paulus_link slug="the-purse" /]
  • c. 58 CE Writes to the Romans. Returns to Jerusalem, undertakes the Temple vow at James's instruction and is arrested. [paulus_link slug="paul-against-the-disciples" anchor="s-playing-the-jew-in-the-temple" /]
  • c. 59–60 CE Held at Caesarea. Appeals to Caesar and is shipwrecked on Malta on the way to Rome. [paulus_link slug="the-roman" anchor="s-the-appeal-to-caesar" /]
  • c. 61–62 CE Under house arrest in Rome.
  • c. 62–67 CE Executed in Rome. Tradition places his death under Nero. [paulus_link slug="the-failed-prophet" /]
