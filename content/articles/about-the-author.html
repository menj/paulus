Mohd Elfie Nieshaem Juferi is a Muslim apologist and a Malay-language author. He wrote the book from which this site draws its case against Paul of Tarsus.

Early life and education

He was born in San Jose, California, in 1980. He grew up in Penang, Malaysia, where he went to Sri Inai Junior and then to SMK Abdullah Munshi.

He studied Computer Science and Information Technology at a private university. He holds an Advanced Diploma in Computing, with a specialism in Software Engineering.

Apologetics

He has written on Islam and Christianity for many years. He co-founded Bismika Allahuma, a website on Islam and its encounter with other faiths, where most of his writing is collected.

On his YouTube channel, The Muslim Apologist, he answers Christian missionaries and the self-described "ex-Muslims" who campaign for apostasy.

Writing and publishing

He writes poetry and fiction in Malay, and his work has won numerous literary awards. Langgam Fikir publishes his books, among them [paulus_link slug="the-book"]the book[/paulus_link] on which this site is based.

Work

He lives in Kuala Lumpur. By trade he works in digital marketing and search engine optimisation.
