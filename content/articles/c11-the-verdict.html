Why do all the manuscripts of the New Testament exist only in Greek, when the language used by ʿĪsā ibn Maryam and his followers was Hebrew or Aramaic?

The absence of any sound textual evidence of the original revelation, together with the existence of copies that contradict one another, confirms that the religion was manipulated from the beginning by irresponsible hands. True is the word of God:
