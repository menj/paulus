This site opened on 30 July 2023. It was meant to carry an English book, published here a chapter at a time as I wrote it.1

The book began in Malay, as a manuscript for print.2 The English version was to be its translation, with chapters added.

I chose to write it in the open. Readers could follow each chapter as it went up, free, and add to it through social media. A PDF of the whole would follow once the book was done, for a small charge to keep the work going.

Its raison d'être has not moved since that first page: to spread the teaching of Islam and defend its principles, on the maxim الكُفْرُ مِلَّةٌ وَاحِدَةٌ (al-kufr millatun wāḥidah), "unbelief, in whatever form, is one creed".

The preface and the first chapter went up that day. A page about the author followed on 20 August 2023.

Both texts are kept, as they first appeared, in [paulus_link slug="the-2023-edition"]The 2023 edition[/paulus_link], among the appendices. The page about the author is now [paulus_link slug="about-the-author"]About the Author[/paulus_link].

The English serial stopped after its first chapter, and I finished the book in Malay. Langgam Fikir published it in August 2025 and reprinted it in October.3

This site now lays out the case that book makes, section by section. [paulus_link slug="the-book"]The book[/paulus_link] has its details.
