Counter-notice

If material you supplied was removed and you believe the removal was a mistake, or that you have the right to use the material, you may send a counter-notice to the same address. Give your name, address and telephone number, identify the material and where it appeared, state in good faith why you believe it was removed in error, and sign the notice.

These procedures follow the notice-and-takedown process of the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act, and they serve equally for notices under Malaysia's Copyright Act 1987.
